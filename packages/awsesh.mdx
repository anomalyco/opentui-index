awsesh uses [OpenTUI Solid](/packages/opentui-solid) to browse SSO profiles,
search AWS accounts and roles, and inspect active credential sessions. You
can open the AWS Console for a selected account or export credentials to
your shell.

The terminal application is available through Homebrew on macOS and Linux.
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/elva-labs/awsesh/releases/latest) also
provide prebuilt binaries for Windows.

The [README](https://github.com/elva-labs/awsesh#interactive-tui) documents
keyboard controls, profile management, and account and role selection.
