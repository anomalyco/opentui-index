Gloomberb is an OpenTUI application for company research and market monitoring.
It shows quotes, charts, financials, filings, news, options, and macroeconomic
data.

It also manages portfolios, watchlists, alerts, notes, and broker connections.
A command bar opens focused panes and arranges them in saved layouts. Live
television needs `mpv` with Kitty video output.

The [Gloomberb documentation](https://gloomberb.com/docs) explains its panes,
commands, workflows, and data access.
