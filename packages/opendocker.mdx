opendocker uses [OpenTUI Solid](/packages/opentui-solid) to show Docker
containers and their status. It streams logs and lets users browse images and
volumes.

The [README](https://github.com/flat6solutions/opendocker#readme) documents
features, platform requirements, and keyboard controls.
